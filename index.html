How We Work

A Proven 4-Phase Intelligence Methodology

Every engagement follows our rigorous intelligence cycle — ensuring airtight evidence, legal compliance, and maximum impact.

01

Briefing & Scoping

We assess your brand's exposure, threat level and key markets. A tailored intelligence brief is developed outlining risks, targets and KPIs.

02

Covert Intelligence

Undercover operatives conduct test purchases, map supply chains and document counterfeit networks across open and wholesale markets.

03

Enforcement Action

Evidence packages are presented to agencies. Joint raids with NAFDAC and police are coordinated, with seizures, arrests and prosecution referrals.

04

Reporting & Recovery

Comprehensive post-operation reports delivered. Ongoing monitoring ensures counterfeit networks do not re-emerge and market health is tracked.
