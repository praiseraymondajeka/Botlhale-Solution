Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can BSA respond to an active counterfeiting crisis?
For confirmed active crises, our rapid response team can begin covert intelligence gathering within 24–48 hours of engagement confirmation. We maintain standing relationships with NAFDAC and state police commands that enable us to move faster than any competitor in the market.
Is my enquiry and company information kept confidential?
Absolutely. Client confidentiality is non-negotiable at BSA. Your identity, the nature of your enquiry, and all case details are never disclosed to any third party. Our operatives are bound by strict NDAs and our internal security protocols mirror those of law enforcement agencies.
Do you only operate in Lagos, or across all of Nigeria?
We have active operational capacity across 12+ Nigerian states including Lagos, Abuja, Kano, Port Harcourt, Ibadan, Aba, and Onitsha. We also operate across West Africa with partner networks in Ghana, Benin, and Côte d'Ivoire.
What industries do you serve?
Our clients span FMCG, pharmaceuticals, beverages, electronics, automotive parts, fashion, personal care, and financial services. If your brand is sold through Nigerian trade channels, we have the expertise and network to protect it.
What does a typical engagement cost?
Every engagement is scoped individually based on the complexity of the threat, geographic spread, and services required. We provide a transparent, itemised proposal before any work begins — no hidden fees. Initial consultations are free and confidential.
Can BSA help with online counterfeiting and social media fraud?
Yes. Our intelligence operations now cover digital channels including social media platforms, e-commerce marketplaces (Jumia, Konga, Facebook Marketplace), and WhatsApp distribution networks — which are increasingly becoming primary routes for counterfeit goods in Nigeria.
Do you work with law enforcement directly?
Yes. BSA has formal Memoranda of Understanding (MoUs) with NAFDAC, the Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Commission (FCCPC), and multiple state police commands. This means enforcement actions we initiate carry full legal weight and can result in arrests, prosecutions, and permanent seizures.
